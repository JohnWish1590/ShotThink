ShotThink Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ShotThink is a Chrome extension that lets you capture and annotate webpage content and, when you choose, send it to a ChatGPT conversation in your own account. This policy explains what the extension handles and where that information goes.

Information ShotThink handles

  • Images you choose to send, including a selected region or a long-page capture, with any annotations you add.
  • Text you select and explicitly send using the context-menu command.
  • Analysis instructions you enter or save in the extension.
  • On this device, task and history details such as status, time, task name, and the resulting ChatGPT conversation link, plus preferences such as language and annotation settings.

When you start a capture, the extension reads the active tab's URL, title, and viewport measurements locally to prepare the capture. It does not separately send the source page URL or title to the developer or to ChatGPT. Page content visible in an image, or included in text you choose, is part of the content you chose to send.

How information is used and stored

ShotThink uses the selected image or text and your instructions to provide its capture-to-ChatGPT feature. Settings and task metadata are stored in Chrome's local extension storage; screenshot image bytes are stored in IndexedDB in your browser profile. This data is kept on your device until you clear the relevant history or remove the extension. ShotThink does not use Chrome Sync for this storage.

You can clear individual task records or the history from the ShotThink options page. Clearing a ShotThink record does not delete a conversation already created in ChatGPT; manage that conversation separately in ChatGPT.

When information is shared

Only when you choose to send, ShotThink passes the selected image or text and your analysis instructions through the ChatGPT website (chatgpt.com) using your signed-in ChatGPT session. OpenAI processes that content under its own terms and privacy policy and the settings for your account. Review OpenAI's Privacy Policy and ChatGPT data controls for details. Content in an image or selected text may include sensitive information, so send only what you intend to share with OpenAI.

The extension does not send your content to a ShotThink-operated server. The developer does not receive it. ShotThink does not sell user data or use it for advertising or analytics.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

ShotThink handles information received from Chrome only as needed to provide its single purpose. Its use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer through ShotThink's GitHub issue tracker.

ShotThink 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

ShotThink 是一款 Chrome 扩展。你可以用它截取并标注网页内容，并在主动选择发送时，将内容发到自己账号中的 ChatGPT 对话。本政策说明扩展处理哪些信息，以及信息会发送到哪里。

ShotThink 处理的信息

  • 你选择发送的图片，包括框选区域或长截图，以及添加的标注。
  • 你选中并通过右键菜单明确发送的文字。
  • 你输入或保存在扩展中的分析说明。
  • 保存在本机的任务和历史信息，例如状态、时间、任务名称、生成的 ChatGPT 对话链接，以及界面语言和标注设置等偏好。

你启动截图时，扩展会在本机读取当前标签页的网址、标题和视口尺寸，以准备截图。扩展不会单独把来源网页的网址或标题发送给开发者或 ChatGPT。截图中可见的网页内容，以及你选择发送的文字，仍属于你主动选择发送的内容。

信息的用途和存储

ShotThink 使用你选中的图片或文字以及分析说明，完成“截图/文字发送到 ChatGPT”功能。设置和任务记录保存在 Chrome 本地扩展存储中；截图图片保存在浏览器配置文件的 IndexedDB 中。数据会留在本机，直到你清理对应记录或移除扩展。ShotThink 不使用 Chrome Sync 同步这些数据。

你可以在 ShotThink 设置页清除单条任务记录或全部历史。清除 ShotThink 记录不会删除已经创建的 ChatGPT 对话；如需删除对话，请在 ChatGPT 中单独管理。

信息何时会被分享

只有在你主动选择发送时，ShotThink 才会通过 chatgpt.com，使用你已登录的 ChatGPT 会话传送所选图片或文字以及分析说明。OpenAI 会依据其自身条款、隐私政策和你的账号设置处理这些内容。详情请查看 OpenAI 隐私政策和 ChatGPT 数据控制说明。图片或选中的文字可能包含敏感信息，请只发送你愿意交给 OpenAI 处理的内容。

扩展不会把你的内容发送到 ShotThink 开发者运营的服务器，开发者不会收到这些内容。ShotThink 不出售用户数据，也不将其用于广告或分析。

Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策

ShotThink 仅在实现其单一用途所必需的范围内处理从 Chrome 获得的信息。ShotThink 对用户数据的使用遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括 Limited Use（有限使用）要求。

联系开发者

如有隐私或支持问题，请通过 ShotThink 的 GitHub Issues 页面联系开发者。
